Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Tend › Settings › Manage Subscription, or go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone.

I bought Tend Plus on another device.
Open Tend › Settings › Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account.

Is my data backed up?
Tend keeps your data on your device only. It is included in your iCloud or computer backups if those are turned on.
