Tend Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026

Tend helps families coordinate care for a relative: medications, appointments, tasks and a care journal. Because that information is personal and often medical, Tend is built so that we never receive it. The short version: Tend does not collect your data.

Information we collect

None. Tend has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking or SDKs. We do not receive, store, share or sell any personal or health information about you or the person you care for.

Information stored on your device

The care profile, circle members, medications, dose logs, appointments, tasks, journal entries and vitals you enter are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never sent to us. They may be included in your own device backups (for example iCloud Backup) if you have those turned on; those backups are controlled by you and by Apple.

You can delete all of it at any time by deleting the app.

Sharing you choose to do

When you tap Share update or share a PDF care summary, Tend creates that text or file on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next (Messages, email, a printer) is your choice, and Tend does not keep a copy or see who receives it.

Purchases

Tend Plus subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple gives us aggregated, anonymous sales reports.

Notifications

If you turn on dose or appointment reminders, Tend asks for permission to send notifications. They are scheduled locally on your device, nothing is sent to a server, and you can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and do not add you to any mailing list. Please don't send medical details by email.

Children

Tend does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If a future version of Tend starts syncing or collecting any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
